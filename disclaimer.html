fcFile Cleaner

Disclaimer

File Cleaner is a privacy and workflow tool. It helps remove file-level metadata from supported images and rename files, but it cannot guarantee how any platform, search engine, marketplace, or detector will classify a file.

What the tool can do

  • Redraw supported JPEG and PNG images to remove embedded metadata from the exported copy.
  • Help remove common EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC, PNG text chunk, and file-level AI metadata from supported images.
  • Rename batches of files before download within your guest allowance or without a daily limit after signing in.

What the tool cannot do

  • Remove visible watermarks or invisible pixel watermarks.
  • Guarantee removal from unsupported formats such as PDF, DOCX, ZIP, video, or audio files.
  • Guarantee that social platforms, marketplaces, or AI detectors will treat a processed image differently.

Use responsibly

Keep original files when you need proof of authorship, licensing, location, chain of custody, or publication history.

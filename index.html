A few things to know

Is everything free?

Yes. Guests can process 3 files per day, resetting at midnight UTC. Each file in a batch counts as one use. People on the same network share the guest allowance. Sign in to a free account for unlimited daily use and batches of up to 500 files. No subscription or payment details required.

Do my files leave my device?

No. File cleanup happens in your browser and downloads are created on your device. Our server handles sign-in and usage limits, but does not receive your files.

What metadata can it remove?

JPEG and PNG images are redrawn as new files. This removes embedded data such as EXIF camera details, GPS tags and PNG text fields, including some AI generation metadata. It does not remove visible or invisible watermarks, or guarantee how an AI detector will classify an image.

Will my images change?

JPEG images are re-encoded at high quality. PNG output is lossless, but browser rendering can change color profiles or other image properties. Your original files stay untouched. Each output downloads separately; your browser may ask you to allow multiple downloads.

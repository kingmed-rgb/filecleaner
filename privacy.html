FCFile Cleaner

Privacy Policy

File Cleaner is designed to process supported files locally in your browser. The app can also use a Vercel backend for free sign-in and guest daily usage limits.

Files you choose

When you select, paste, or drag files into the app, your browser reads them locally so it can create processed downloads. JPEG and PNG images can be redrawn to remove embedded metadata. Other file types are renamed only.

Uploads and storage

The app does not intentionally upload your selected files to a File Cleaner server, and it does not store copies of your files. Downloads are created on your device by your browser.

Accounts and usage

We store your email address and, if you use Google sign-in, your name and profile image. A session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Signing out removes that cookie from this browser.

Guests can process 3 images per UTC day. We use a keyed hash of your network address to enforce this allowance and limit login attempts; raw network addresses are not stored in our usage database. Guest usage records expire within 48 hours. People sharing a network may share the guest allowance. Free accounts have unlimited daily usage.

Login challenges and attempt counters expire after 10 minutes. Account details remain stored to support future sign-ins. Our hosting and email providers may also retain operational logs.

Advertising

Google AdSense is our intended source of revenue. Ads are currently disabled until our publisher account is configured. When enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies and device information to serve and measure ads, subject to applicable consent choices. Signing in does not remove advertising.

Learn how Google uses information at Google’s partner sites policy. We do not collect payment details or sell subscriptions.

Limits

Browser-based metadata removal cannot remove visible marks, invisible watermarks, or information baked into pixels. It only works on file-level metadata for supported image formats.
